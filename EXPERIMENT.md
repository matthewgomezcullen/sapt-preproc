# 1. Hypothesis

H1-classical: Conditional on DiffDock-L containing at least one near-native pose, a SAPT0 interaction score adds pose-ranking information beyond the DiffDock-L's confidence model.
H1-quantum: Replacing SAPT0 first-order electrostatic/exchange terms by an ($N_{e}$e, $N_{o}$o or smaller) active-space correlated estimate changes rankings in a direction that improves near-native selection. Ideal VQE reproduces the same CASCI correction closely enough.

## 1.2. Estimand

Conditional Top-1 Reranking Success: success only among complexes with at least one near-native (RMSD $\le$ 2 Å) sampled pose.

# 4. Metrics

- Top-1 and Top-3 success.
- Rank relation.
- Pairwise discrimination.
- Operational feasibility.

Note different forms of failure:

- Generator failure: No near-native pose generated.
- Validity failure: The near-native pose is physically invalid.
- **Ranker** failure: near-native, physically valid pose not selected.
